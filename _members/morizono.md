Marc received his undergraduate degree in Chemistry and Chemical Biology from Cornell University where he worked in the lab of Nozomi Ando to understand the mechanisms underlying oxygen-sensitive metalloproteins using X-ray crystallography methods. As a graduate student at the University of California San Diego, Marc earned his PhD in the lab of Mark Herzik and used single-particle cryoEM to reveal the structure of mitochondrial Hsp70, in complex with its co-chaperone GrpEL1, providing molecular details into mitochondrial protein import.

As a joint postdoc between the Grotjahn and Cravatt labs, Marc is interested in using fluorescence microscopy, cellular assays, and cryo-electron tomography to understand how small-molecule modulators of mitochondrial-ER contact sites impact cellular and mitochondrial biology. 

Outside of the lab, Marc enjoys doing drawing, heading to the beach to relax or surf, and trying out new foods.
