José M. Borbolla Salinas earned his undergraduate degree in Biomedical Sciences from the University of Washington Tacoma. During his time there, he worked in the Baughman Lab, where he developed a purification protocol for the NFkB transcription factors p50/RelA and p50/RelB. His focus was on maintaining their intrinsically disordered regions while working with a full functional construct.

Currently, José serves as a Research Technician in the Grotjahn Lab. In this role, he is actively working to enhance his computational and microscopy skills, with the ultimate goal of pursuing a graduate degree in biochemistry.

Outside of the lab, José enjoys visiting zoos and museums. He also engages in various physical activities, including playing fútbol and cycling.
